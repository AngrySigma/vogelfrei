# Planned chapters

Rules Vogelfrei does not have yet, to be adapted from *LotFP Rules & Magic*
(2013). Source text: `~/lotfp/rules/04-maritime.md`,
`~/lotfp/rules/06-property-and-finance.md` and the Artillery section of
`~/lotfp/rules/12-firearms.md`. Nothing here is decided; each section ends
with the questions to settle before writing. Where there is a proposal, it is
marked as one.

Smaller open design questions live in [NOTES.md](NOTES.md).

## Maritime

### What Vogelfrei has now

- Ship prices only: [Vehicles](docs/Equipment/Vehicles.md) and
  [Antiquated Vehicles](docs/Equipment/Antiquated%20Vehicles.md). No crew,
  speed, cargo or hull figures.
- A [Sailing](docs/Adventuring/Skills.md#sailing) skill with a one-line
  description and no mechanics.
- A stub [Sailor](docs/Character/Classes/Ranger/Careers/Sailor.md) career.

### What LotFP has

- **Ships:** 17 vessels, raft to frigate, each with required crew, miles per
  day sailing and rowing, cargo in tons, and Ship Hit Points (SHP). Quality
  changes speed and SHP: Good +5%, Excellent +10%, Advanced +15%, Poor −10%,
  Awful −25%.
- **SHP:** 1 SHP = 10 ordinary points of damage; a hit under 10 does nothing
  to the hull. At 0 SHP the ship stops, its weapons fail, and it sinks in
  1d10 rounds.
- **Rivers:** the day's distance changes by 1d8+4 miles with or against a
  strong current; shallows, rapids, sandbars at the Referee's call.
- **Wind:** 2d6 each morning. 2 no wind (sailing ships stuck, galleys row);
  3–5 movement −¾/−½/−¼; 6–8 normal; 9–10 +¼/+½; 11 ×2 with a 10% chance of
  shipping water (20% galleys: half movement, 3d6 SHP); 12 storm, ×3 in a
  random direction, 5d6 SHP, galleys sink 80% unless they reach harbour, a
  ship driven onto land finds shelter 25% of the time or is wrecked.
- **Crew:** under full crew a ship loses speed and takes 1d6 SHP a day:
  over ¾ crew −25% speed *or* 1d6 SHP; ¾–½ −50%; ½–¼ −75%; under ¼ it drifts.
- **Encounters:** monsters can surprise a ship, a ship never surprises a
  monster; distance 4d6 × 10 yards.
- **Chases:** escape 80% if faster, 50% at equal speed, −10% per 30' slower
  (minimum 10%). A failed escape lets the pursuer close 10 yards a round, or
  its full speed if more than 30' faster.
- **Repairs:** half a crew repairs 1 SHP a day, a full crew 2. Below half SHP
  a ship cannot be repaired past half without a port; below a quarter, not
  past a quarter.
- **Catapults:** 150–300 yards, attack as a Fighter of level = crew working
  it, fire every 5/8/10 rounds with 4/3/2 crew, 3d6 SHP or 1d6 SHP of fire
  per Turn; 5 crew need 3 Turns to put a fire out.
- **Rams:** small ship (1d4+4) × 10, large ship (1d6+5) × 10; against large
  sea monsters 3d8 / 6d6 hit points.
- **Boarding:** automatic if both sides want it, 35% if only one does;
  boarders take −2 to attack and AC.
- **Artillery** (Firearms chapter): 250 sp per size category; against
  individuals it needs a natural 20 and the target still saves versus Breath;
  1d6 SHP per size category; fires once per Turn per size category.

### Carries over with light edits

The wind table, storms and wrecks, the crew thresholds, repairs, boarding (now
with Guard, Press and Defensive Fighting), and the quality modifiers
(Vehicles already has them). SHP fits the damage model: a ship is like a
monster with no Stamina, and with d8 weapons "10 points per SHP" means small
arms cannot hurt a hull.

### Questions

1. **Watches at sea.** *Proposal:* a sailing ship keeps going through the
   night with the crew in shifts, so its day is **six watches of sailing**
   and no rest watches; galleys need rested rowers and use active watches
   like a party on land. Is that right? Do passengers (the party) still need
   their sleep watch while the ship sails?
2. **Speed units.** Give ships miles per watch (fits the land rules) or keep
   miles per day? The river adjustment (1d8+4 miles a day) becomes per watch
   either way.
3. **Core-period ships need stats.** LotFP covers only the antiquated ones
   (trireme, quadrireme, longship, cog, caravel, carrack) and the brig and
   corvette that are already marked anachronistic. Crew, speed, cargo and SHP
   must be written for: shallop, riverboat, river galley, sailboat, sloop,
   pinnace, ketch, brigantine, fluyt, galleon, frigate, ship of the line.
   Base them on historical tonnage and complement, or scale from LotFP's
   nearest ship?
4. **LotFP's galleon** has 104 SHP and only 150 tons of cargo, against the
   carrack's 685; likely a misprint. Ignore it when writing ours?
5. **Cannon instead of catapults.** Firearms add no Ballistic Skill in
   Vogelfrei, so "attacks as a Fighter of level X" has no equivalent.
   *Proposal:* a gun hits on d20 + situational modifiers like any firearm;
   crew size sets the reload time, as Ballistic Skill does for a musket;
   damage 1d6 SHP per size category from LotFP's artillery. Keep fire (the
   catapult's pitch) as heated shot or fire pots? Keep catapults at all, for
   antiquated ships only?
6. **Rams.** Only galleys ram in this period. Keep the rule for galleys and
   antiquated ships only?
7. **Chases.** LotFP's percentages do not match [Flee!](docs/Encounters/Combat%20Actions.md#flee)
   (contested d20 + movement ÷ 10 + Agility). *Proposal:* the same contested
   roll with the ship's speed, adding the captain's Sailing in place of
   Agility. Agree?
8. **What does Sailing do?** Candidates: navigation and getting lost at sea
   (as Bushcraft on land), finding harbour in a storm (instead of the flat
   25%), catching the wind in a chase, the wind roll itself. Which of these?
9. **Encounter distance.** LotFP gives 4d6 × 10 yards; Vogelfrei's
   [Encounter Distance](docs/Encounters/Encounter%20Distance.md) is in feet.
   A separate sea distance, or reuse the land one?
10. **Sea monsters.** Ram damage against monsters is in hit points. Monsters
    are the Referee's to stat, so drop the monster figures, or keep them as a
    guide?
11. **Where it goes and which tier.** A new page in Adventuring (beside
    Wilderness Travel) gated to Expert? Base?
12. **Who it needs.** Hiring a crew: does it use the
    [Retainers](docs/Retainers/index.md) rules (sailors and marines as
    retainers with wages and morale), and does the Sailor career get its
    trait from this chapter?

- The [Sailor](docs/Character/Classes/Ranger/Careers/Sailor.md) progression

## Property and Investment

### What Vogelfrei has now

- [Lodging](docs/Equipment/Lodging.md): inns, and rent at 30 sp (city) /
  15 sp (rural) per 10' square a month; buying costs a hundred times the
  monthly rent. No other property prices.
- [Retainers](docs/Retainers/index.md): live-in wages and space requirements
  per retainer, and the household staff in the
  [Expanded Retainer List](docs/Retainers/Expanded%20Retainer%20List.md),
  including an Accountant: a household of five or more retainers without one
  pays 1d20% more running costs each month.
- [Advancement](docs/Character/Advancement.md): tax income, trade and
  commerce earn money but no XP.

### What LotFP has

- **Property:** owning land means a stake in local politics, not rulership;
  ruling is outside the rules.
- **Upkeep:** 1 gp (50 sp) a month per resident for food and essentials.
  Maintenance 1d6% of the property's value a year, +1d10% with no accountant
  on staff, +1d4% with no handyman or craftsman.
- **Taxes:** 1d4+3% of the property's value a year, +1d10% with no
  accountant.
- **Investment:** the player names a sum and a risk; the character does not
  run the business. Yearly return: stable 1d8−4%, risky 1d20−10%, wild
  1d100−50%; +1d10% with an accountant. A gain is paid out in cash; a loss
  reduces the investment. The die explodes both ways: on its maximum roll
  again and add, on its minimum roll again and subtract, as long as it keeps
  coming up. Withdrawing outside the yearly return costs 1d6+4%. Figures
  include tax.
- **Bankruptcy:** each year a stable investment goes bust 5% of the time,
  risky 10%, wild 25%, losing everything in it.

### Carries over with light edits

Almost all of it: the percentages are yearly, so the watches rework does not
touch them, and Vogelfrei uses the same 1 gp = 50 sp. Upkeep becomes 50 sp a
month per resident. The XP rule already excludes investment returns.

### Questions

1. **Two accountant rules.** Vogelfrei's (+1d20% monthly running costs for a
   household of 5+ without one) and LotFP's (+1d10% maintenance, +1d10%
   taxes without one; +1d10% investment return with one) overlap. Keep both,
   merge them into one, or replace ours with LotFP's?
2. **Upkeep vs retainer wages.** LotFP's 50 sp per resident a month and our
   live-in wages ("room and board included") may count food twice. Does a
   live-in retainer cost the household 50 sp on top of the wage, or is board
   inside the wage?
3. **Property prices.** All we have is rent × 100. Add a price list of period
   property (a townhouse, a shop with workshop, a mill, a farm, a manor, a
   fortified house), or keep the rent formula only?
4. **Status.** Careers carry Brass/Silver/Gold Status. Should it touch
   property: Gold characters exempt from some taxes (nobility), Brass ones
   unable to buy land in some places, or no link?
5. **Careers.** Should the Merchant and Scion (Townsman, both stubs) or the
   Accountant retainer get a trait from this: a bonus to returns, a lower
   bankruptcy chance, cheaper upkeep?
6. **Exploding returns.** Keep the explode-both-ways rule? On a wild
   investment the d100 can run away (rare but large). Cap it, or keep the
   swing as the point of a wild investment?
7. **Time.** Investments pay once a year. Does checking on them or moving
   money take a [watch in town](docs/Adventuring/Wilderness%20Travel.md),
   like any errand, or is it off-screen?
8. **Where it goes and which tier.** Its own page, in the Retainers chapter
   (which already holds the household staff) or in Adventuring? Expert only?
