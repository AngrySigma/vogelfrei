# Vogelfrei

> *To be **vogelfrei** is to be cast out, beyond the protection of kings and gods alike: free to be hunted, betrayed, and forgotten — and free to forge your own destiny.*

**Vogelfrei** is an old-school (OSR) tabletop role-playing game set in the age of pike and shot, from Pavia to the end of the Thirty Years' War. Powder and plague, confessional slaughter, ships bound for a New World — and magic that is real, rare, and feared. You play outcasts and fortune-seekers who leave law and custom behind for the wilds, ruins, and depths. Wealth is the aim, death is always close, and cunning counts for more than courage.

**[Read the rules online →](https://angrysigma.github.io/vogelfrei/)**

## What to expect

- **Deadly, unfair combat.** Fights are war, not sport, and never mandatory.
- **Treasure is the goal.** Experience comes from wealth won, not monsters slain.
- **Birth and career matter.** Each character has a class (Warrior, Magic-User, Cleric, Rogue, Ranger, Peasant, Academic, Townsman, or one of the demihumans) and a career within it that says who they were before they took to the road.
- **Hired help.** Retainers carry torches, hold the line, and die in your stead.
- **Random tables everywhere.** Let the dice surprise you.
- **Problems, not puzzles.** Expect situations with no intended solution — or with several.

## Pick your complexity

The book comes in three views, switched with the eye button in the site's header:

- **Simple** — the four classic classes, core combat and equipment. Start here.
- **Base** — the full game.
- **Advanced** — adds detailed hazards, magical and holy activities, the Phoenix class, and period curiosities.
