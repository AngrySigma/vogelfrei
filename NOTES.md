# Open design questions

Things noticed while fixing rules inconsistencies that need a design decision,
not a correction. Whole chapters still to write (maritime, property and
investment) are in [PLANNED-CHAPTERS.md](PLANNED-CHAPTERS.md).

## The zweihander problem

The **Great** weapon category covers two-handed swords, mauls and great axes
as one line: d8 high, armour modifier 0, length 4, 50sp.

That flattens a real distinction. A zweihander takes genuine instruction to use
and is useless for forcing a door. A greataxe needs far less training and will
happily chop one down — the Doors rule explicitly calls for "some sort of axe
for a wooden door". Both are Great weapons, so the rules cannot currently tell
them apart.

Options, none applied:

- Split **Great** into two entries (bladed vs hafted) with different Training
  marks and door-breaking behaviour.
- Keep one line and hang the difference off the *specific* weapon the player
  names, since the generic categories already require naming an actual weapon.
- Add a small weapon-property tag set (Training, something for
  door-breaking) and let each named weapon carry its own tags.

## Smaller open items

- **Blessing names.** Blessings 3 and 18 are still called *Charisma* and
  *Wisdom*, stats the game does not have. Their effects (+1 Leadership, +1
  Intelligence) are correct and were left alone; only the names are odd.

- **Status is unset on 41 of 56 career pages** (the stubs), so those
  characters cannot roll starting money. Deferred by request.

## Spells named in the text but missing from the lists

The LotFP import (Magic-User 1–9, Cleric 1–7) left these spell names in the
text without a page of their own. Each one either needs a page or a rewrite of
the sentence that names it.

- **Ventriloquism**, named in Permanency (MU 8): "the following spells can be
  made permanent on a creature: … Ventriloquism". There is no such spell.
- **Wish**, named in Symbol (MU 8). There is no such spell.
- **Cause Fear**, named in Spell Immunity (Cleric 4), is the reverse of Remove
  Fear (Cleric 1) but is not linked there.
- **True Seeing** and **Remove Curse** exist only as Cleric miracles, but
  Magic-User spells rely on them: Veil, Simulacrum and Duo-Dimension (True
  Seeing); Weird Vortex and Feeblemind (Remove Curse). The links point at the
  Cleric pages. Decide whether Magic-Users get their own versions.
- **Reverse spells without a page.** These live inside their parent spell and
  links go there: Slow (Haste), Cause Disease (Cure Disease), Flesh to Stone
  (Stone to Flesh), Cause Fear (Remove Fear), Harm (Heal), Freedom (Imprisonment).
