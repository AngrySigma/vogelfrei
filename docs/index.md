> *To be **vogelfrei** is to be cast out, beyond the protection of kings and gods alike, free in the most terrible way: free to be hunted, betrayed, and forgotten — and free to forge your own destiny.*

**Vogelfrei** is a tabletop role-playing game of an early-Renaissance world: pikes, pistols, and plague, where magic is real, rare, and feared. Birth and career define you in town; beyond the walls, nothing protects you at all. You play outcasts and fortune-seekers who leave law and custom behind for the wilds, ruins, and depths — wealth is the aim, death is always close, and cunning counts for more than courage.

## The Play

- Combat is war, not sport: deadly for everyone, rarely fair, and never mandatory.
- Adventures happen in dungeons, ruins, and the wilds between them.
- Treasure is the goal: experience comes from wealth won, not monsters slain.
- Random tables drive much of the world, so let the dice surprise you.
- Expect problems with no intended solution — or with several.

## Players

- Your character is more than the sheet: describe what you do, not which rule you use.
- A good plan beats a good roll, so scout, prepare, and stack the odds before you commit.
- Stuck? Read your inventory — the answer is often already in your pack.
- Pay attention to the world and take notes: information is leverage, and it can save your life.
- Losses will come. Retreat, regroup, and let the story grow around the scars.

## The Referee

- The table is yours: there is no one right way to run the game.
- Judge impartially — the world reacts, it does not root for or against anyone.
- Don't hoard what matters: telegraph danger and give information freely.
- Let their choices kill them, never your whims.
- Reward what veteran players know. They earned that knowledge at another table's cost.

Much has been written on this style of play; the essential read is [Principia Apocrypha](https://lithyscaphe.blogspot.com/p/principia-apocrypha.html).
