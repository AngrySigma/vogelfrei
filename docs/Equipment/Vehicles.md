# Vehicles

Prices buy a sound, workaday vehicle with ordinary fittings. Draft animals, crew, cannon, provisions, cargo, and port fees are separate. Ship categories are intentionally broad: rig, burden, and local name vary from one coast to another.

| Kind | Vehicle | Cost |  | Typical use |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
|  |  | City | Rural |  |
| Land | Handcart | 15sp | 10sp | One person; expedition gear or market goods |
| Land | Cart | 50sp | 25sp | Two wheels; one draft animal |
| Land | Coach | 100sp | - | Passengers on maintained roads |
| Land | Wagon | 160sp | 75sp | Four wheels; freight, settlers, or baggage |
| Land | Sled or sledge | 30sp | 20sp | Snow, ice, mud, or dragging a heavy load |

Older and later vehicle types retained from previous equipment lists appear under [Antiquated Vehicles](Antiquated%20Vehicles.md).

!!! tip "Quality"
    Good-quality vehicles cost 5% more. Excellent vehicles cost 15% more. Advanced construction costs 33% more. Poor vehicles cost 10% less, and awful vehicles cost 25% less. Quality changes reliability and comfort, not the historical period of the design.
