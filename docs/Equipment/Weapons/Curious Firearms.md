# Curious Firearms

This page keeps rare experiments and displaced equipment available to Advanced campaigns without presenting them as ordinary purchases of the age. The Referee decides whether any particular example exists. Unless stated otherwise, these weapons use the loading, lock, [Proof](../Armor.md#proof), fouling, training, and misfire rules from [Firearms](Firearms.md).

## Period Curiosities

| Firearm | Damage | Range (S/M/L) | Cost | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| ***Double-barrelled gun (2H)*** | d10 | 30'—60'—300' | 100sp | Two separate loaded barrels; choosing and firing one takes an action |
| ***Gun axe (2H)*** | d8 | 30'—60'—300' | 80sp | Functions as a Medium melee weapon |
| ***Gun halberd (2H)*** | d8 | 30'—60'—300' | 120sp | Functions as a Polearm in melee |
| *Plug bayonet* | d4 | - | 5sp | Appears only in the age's final years, made for one gun; see below |
| Magazine pistol | d8 | 20'—40'—80' | 750sp | A unique manually operated repeating mechanism holding 1d6 shots |
| ***Magazine gun (2H)*** | d8 | 30'—60'—300' | 600sp | A unique manually operated repeating mechanism holding 1d8 shots |
| *Grenade* | d6 | 10'—20'—30' | 15sp | Thrown; see below |

A magazine weapon can fire on successive Rounds, but its mechanism must be manually worked between shots and takes a full Turn to reload. On any misfire it jams and requires an [Engineering](../../Adventuring/Skills.md#engineering) roll plus one Turn to clear. Failure damages the mechanism until repaired in a workshop.

A plug bayonet counts as a Minor weapon by itself. Fitting it takes 1 Round, blocks the muzzle so the gun cannot fire, and lets a musket function as a Spear. Removing it also takes 1 Round.

A **grenade** is a hollow iron shell filled with corned powder and closed with a wooden fuse, thrown by hand. Throughout this age it is a siege and boarding weapon, issued to men chosen for a particular piece of work rather than sold across a counter; the companies of grenadiers who carry them as a matter of course belong to a later generation of soldiers than any of these. Lighting the fuse requires an open flame or a lit match and takes 1 Round. On the Round after it is thrown, everyone within 10' saves versus Paralyzation or takes d6 damage; the thrower chooses to hold it one extra Round to deny the enemy time to throw it back, at the risk the Referee thinks that deserves. A grenade ignores armour entirely — [Proof](../Armor.md#proof) is no help against a blast — but [cover](../../Encounters/Combat%20Actions.md#cover) is.

## Outside the Period

The following legacy entries are preserved for campaigns that deliberately admit later inventions. They are not available in a game that keeps strictly to the age.

| Firearm | Damage | Range (S/M/L) | Cost | Why it is here |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| ***Hand mortar (2H)*** | d8 | 25'—50'—200' | 1000sp | A specialist siege weapon of the generation after this one; see below |
| Duck-foot pistol | d6 | 10'—20'—50' | 200sp | A later multi-barrel form firing in a fan |
| Pepperbox | d8 | 20'—40'—80' | 100sp | The familiar revolving pepperbox is a 19th-century weapon |
| Recoil-rotating repeater pistol | d8 | 20'—40'—80' | 150sp | Its self-rotation by recoil is outside period technology |
| ***Recoil-rotating repeater gun (2H)*** | d8 | 30'—60'—300' | 120sp | Its self-rotation by recoil is outside period technology |
| Cane pistol | d8 | 10'—20'—50' | 120sp | Kept as a later concealed weapon |
| ***Long rifle (2H)*** | d12 | 100'—200'—1200' | 800sp | Kept as the former exaggerated rifle entry |

When firing a hand mortar, the target number is always AC 15. On a miss, the shot scatters in a random direction three feet for every point the attack total fell short of 15, to a maximum of half the distance to the target. Everyone within 30' of the landing point saves versus Paralyzation or takes 1d8 damage. Substantial cover may modify the save.

A Duck-foot pistol fires all barrels together. Every target in the fan, up to the number of barrels, saves versus Paralyzation or takes d6 damage; range penalties modify the save. A Pepperbox or legacy repeater holds three shots by default, with each additional barrel adding half the base cost. After each shot it may bring the next loaded barrel into use and fire again on the following Round.
