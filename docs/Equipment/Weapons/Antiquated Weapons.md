# Antiquated Weapons

These weapons survive from ancient or late-medieval fighting traditions. They can turn up in old armouries, remote regions, collections, tombs, or deliberately archaic campaigns, but they are not ordinary 17th-century purchases.

| Weapon | Damage | Length | Cost |  |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| *Cestus* | d3 | 0 | 10sp | - |
| ***Mancatcher (2H)***\* | d6 | 2 | 20sp | - |

| ***Longbow*** | d6 | - | 45sp | - |

The Longbow uses ranges 50'—600'—900'. It requires training and uses the ammunition rules on the [Ranged Weapons](Ranged%20Weapons.md) page. All other asterisked entries use the training rule on the [Melee Weapons](Melee%20Weapons.md) page.

| Weapon | Effects |
| --- | --- |
| Lance | May be used one-handed while charging on horseback. On foot it functions as a pike and may Brace. |
| Mancatcher | On a hit, the victim must save versus Paralyzation or become helpless. The wielder is also helpless against other attacks while holding the victim. |
| Weighted net | On a hit, the victim must save versus Paralyzation or become helpless. The victim may save again each Round. |

Great weapons include two-handed swords, mauls, and great axes. Halflings cannot wield Great weapons, lances, or comparable arms.
