!!! info "Training"
    The weapons marked **Training** do nothing in untaught hands. A character with no plausible background with one must spend time learning it before it can be used effectively; the Referee decides what a character's background already covers. Rocks and ordinary thrown weapons need no instruction — anyone can throw a hatchet badly.

!!! info "Range"
    Medium range imposes a -2 penalty to hit; Long range imposes -4.

!!! note "Ammunition"
    Arrows and crossbow bolts cost **5bp** each. Sling bullets cost **2bp** each. A quiver and up to twenty arrows or bolts count as one item for encumbrance.

### Weapon Traits

| Weapon | Effects |
| --- | --- |
| Light crossbow | Fire every other Round; ignore 1 Armor Rating. |
| Heavy crossbow | Fire every third Round; ignore 3 Armor Rating. |
| Sling | May use stones as ammunition, halving effective range. |

A crossbow is not a firearm and does not care about [Proof](../Armor.md#proof): it reduces Armor Rating by the amount listed and the rest applies normally.

The military longbow belongs to a previous age of war and appears under [Antiquated Weapons](Antiquated%20Weapons.md). Hunting bows, and the bows of peoples who never gave them up, use the ordinary Bow entry above.
