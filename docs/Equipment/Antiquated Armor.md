# Antiquated Armor

These defenses belong to earlier warfare. They may survive as heirlooms, old colonial stores, grave goods, tournament harness, or equipment in a deliberately archaic region, but they are not ordinary purchases in the 17th-century game.

Use ordinary AR and Shot AR exactly as described on the main [Armor](Armor.md) page. An ordinary full harness is excellent against hand weapons but is not therefore bulletproof. A surviving harness deliberately reinforced and proofed against shot may have Shot AR 1 at the Referee's discretion, but it is exceptionally heavy and rare.

A pavise protects against non-firearm missiles while carried. Planted and properly braced, it is terrain and may instead provide cover; whether it stops a bullet depends on its construction.
