**Armor Rating (AR)** is added to Armor Class against melee attacks, bows, crossbows, thrown weapons, and similar dangers.

**Shot AR** is used instead of AR against firearms. A value of 0 means that the armour gives no AC against a shot; it may still turn a graze or explain how Stamina prevented a mortal wound. Shot AR is never added to ordinary AR.

Pikeman's armour comprises a helmet, breast- and backplate, and tassets. Harquebusier armour combines a buff coat, helmet, and proofed breast- and backplate. Three-quarter cuirassier armour is a heavy, proofed harness protecting the wearer to the knees. A proof dent is evidence that a plate survived a test shot, not a promise against every gun at every distance.

Shield bonuses do not apply against firearms. A planted mantlet, stout wall, or other substantial barrier instead grants [cover](../Encounters/Combat%20Actions.md#cover).

Armour pieces do not stack: use the best complete protection actually worn. For the full attack procedure, refer to [Combat Actions](../Encounters/Combat%20Actions.md#attack). Older harnesses and defenses appear under [Antiquated Armor](Antiquated%20Armor.md).
