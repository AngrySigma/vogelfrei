# Pyromancer

| Level | New traits           | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
| ----- | -------------------- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1     | Literate             | 1   | —   | —   |
| 2     | +1 Lore (Fire)       | 2   | —   | —   |
| 3     | —                    | 2   | 1   | —   |
| 4     | —                    | 2   | 2   | —   |
| 5     | —                    | 2   | 2   | 1   |
| 6     | —                    | 2   | 2   | 2   |

The numbered columns show how many spells of each spell level the Pyromancer may keep memorized at one time. See [Spells](../Special%20Rules/Spells.md) for how spells are recorded, prepared, and cast.

A Pyromancer begins literate. At 2nd level, they gain one advance in [Lore](../../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#lore), specialized in fire, combustion, and the occult virtues of heat.
