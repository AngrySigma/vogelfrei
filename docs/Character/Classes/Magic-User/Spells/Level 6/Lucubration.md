# Lucubration

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 6  
**Duration**: Instantaneous  
**Range**: 0  

This spell captures the energy of a previously cast spell and restores it to the caster. Any one spell of 5th level or lower cast within the past 24 hours is again fully prepared and ready to cast.
