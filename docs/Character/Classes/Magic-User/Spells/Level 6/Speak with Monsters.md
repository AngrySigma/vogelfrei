# Speak with Monsters

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 6  
**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: 30'  

The caster can comprehend and communicate with any creature. The caster can ask questions of, and receive answers from, one particular kind of monster, although the spell does not make it any more friendly or cooperative than normal. If a creature is friendly toward the caster, it may do him some favour or service.
