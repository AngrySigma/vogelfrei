# Phantasmal Supergoria

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 6  
**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: 10'/level  

This spell creates a creature the caster has previously seen that will obey his every mental command. However, it only exists in the minds of those seeing, smelling, or hearing it. The first time a phantasmal creature causes damage, the victim receives a saving throw versus Magic. Success means the creature ceases to exist as far as that character is concerned and no damage is taken; otherwise, the illusion is as deadly as any real creature. If the saving throw is not made, then another save is allowed only after the illusory creature has taken so much “damage” that it must be dead although the illusion can take no actual damage because it does not exist.

Saving throws are also granted if a character disbelieves a certain situation created by the illusion.
