# Shades

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 6  
**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: 30'  

The caster creates phantasmal pseudo-real monsters in an area of 20'. The monster or monsters created cannot exceed the caster’s level in Hit Dice. Monsters created in this fashion must all be the same type. They each have 5 hp per the creature’s normal Hit Dice. Victims are allowed a saving throw versus Magic to realize that the creatures are only partly real. The phantasmal monsters are able to attack and deal damage as per a normal creature of their type to any being that fails this saving throw. If the saving throw succeeds, the phantasmal monsters have an effective AC 15 and only inflict half of their normal damage.
