# Weird Vortex

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 6  
**Duration**: See Below  
**Range**: 120'  

As this spell is cast, a great storm of light whips around the target and produces a random effect. Roll a 1d20 and consult the following table:

1. A 30' radius fog appears around the target for 1d4+2 Rounds. All plant material within the fog dies instantly, and all living beings within it take 1d4 damage per Round.
2. The lightstorm electrifies, frying the target for 1d6 damage per caster level. Everyone within 20' is also struck by lightning bolts for half the number of dice of damage as the main target. All victims suffer half damage if they make a saving throw versus Breath Weapon.
3. The target ages 2d20 years, save versus Magic for half effect.
4. The target's mind is melded with the mind of a Magic-User level 1d10+4, with a full load of (randomly determined) spells. The target can cast any of these spells, but cannot re-memorize them; when they are gone, they are gone.
5. The target begins dancing uncontrollably for 2d6 Rounds, during which time he can take no other action but the dancing. The target will automatically fail any saving throws during this time.
6. A duplicate of the target appears, with the same current Wounds (and spells, if any) and equipment and will fight the target to the death.
7. The subject becomes immaterial, invisible, and silent to all but the caster. Forever. Or until dispelled.
8. A duplicate of the target appears, with the same current Wounds (and spells, if any) and equipment. The duplicate will be of the same mind and attitudes as the original, and a fast and firm ally.
9. The target is infected with a Poverty Curse. He must divest himself of all wealth (including luxury or magical items) within 6 Turns or die. If he ever comes in contact with any of his discarded wealth, he will die instantly.
10. A fleshy umbilical cord forms attaching the caster and target. Their Wounds are then added together to form a common pool; when one dies so does the other. Only a [Remove Curse](../../../Cleric/Miracles/Level%203/Remove%20Curse.md) can dissolve the cord and give each character their proper individuality back.
11. The target develops explosive blood. When struck in melee, the target's attackers suffer 1d4 damage for every point of damage they inflict. This lasts until the target has suffered in total the same amount of Wounds as he had when this spell was cast on him.
12. The target gains 2d100 temporary Wounds, but will never succeed at a saving throw until the temporary Wounds are lost.
13. The target becomes hyper-evolved, suffering a –2 penalty to his Toughness, Agility, and Strength modifiers, and gains a +4 adjustment to his Intelligence modifier and a +2 bonus to his Willpower modifier.
14. The target de-evolves, suffering a –2 penalty to his Leadership, Intelligence, and Willpower modifiers, and gains a +3 adjustment to his Toughness modifier, a +1 bonus to his Agility modifier, and a +2 bonus to his Strength modifier.
15. Food becomes poisonous to the target; only by consuming 10 gold pieces every day can he sustain himself.
16. Two vials appear, one red, one blue, halfway between the caster and target. If both the caster and target toast, one dies (no save), the other gains a level. Which character gets which result is entirely random.
17. The caster comes under the total mental domination of the target.
18. The target's skin becomes as hard as stone. He gains AC 22 and 3d6 extra Wounds. When the extra Wounds are used up (these extra Wounds, as all other “extra” Wounds, cannot be healed), the stone covering falls off.
19. A 10 Hit Dice extra-dimensional creature materializes between the caster and target. It will serve as a staunch ally to one of them and an enemy to the other, 50%/50% chance for each.
20. A 10 Hit Dice extra-dimensional creature materializes between the caster and the target. It is in fact an illusion, as per [Phantasmal Supergoria](Phantasmal%20Supergoria.md), but not under anyone’s control, and nobody realizes initially that it is but an illusion. It is on one side or the other, 50%/50% chance for each.
