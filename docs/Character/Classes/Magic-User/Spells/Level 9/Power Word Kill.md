# Power Word Kill

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 9  
**Duration**: Instantaneous  
**Range**: 2.5'/caster level  

The caster utters a single word of power that instantly kills one or more creatures within a 20' diameter, whether the creatures can hear the word or not. This spell will kill multiple creatures if they have less than 11 Wounds each, or the spell will kill a single creature that has 60 Wounds or less. The caster must choose whether he is attempting to kill one creature or multiple creatures when he casts the spell. If multiple creatures are targeted, a maximum of 120 Wounds’ total of creatures can be killed. Any creature that has 61 or more Wounds is unaffected by Power Word Kill. There is no saving throw against this spell.
