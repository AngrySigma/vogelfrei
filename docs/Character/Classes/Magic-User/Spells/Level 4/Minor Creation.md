# Minor Creation

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 4  
**Duration**: Instantaneous  
**Range**: Touch  

The caster creates an object of nonliving vegetable matter (rope, cloth, wood, etc.). The volume of the item created cannot exceed 1' cube per caster level. A tiny piece of matter of the same sort of item the caster plans to create must be used when casting Minor Creation. Thus, within the caster’s limits on the item’s volume, he could create a basket from a piece of straw, a door or club from a splinter of wood, a cloak from a piece of wool, etc.
