# Mnemonic Enhancer

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 4  
**Duration**: Instantaneous  
**Range**: 0  

This spell allows the caster to prepare additional spells or retain spells recently cast. Up to 3 levels of spells could be retained, or memorized above and beyond the number normally available for the caster’s level. This can be in any combination of spell levels. For instance, one 3rd level spell or three 1st level spells. If spells are to be retained, then rather than memorize extra spells, Mnemonic Enhancer is cast first and then used to discharge the spells of the stated spell levels, while retaining them for one more casting.
