# Seven Gates

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 4  
**Duration**: 4d6 Turns  
**Range**: See Below  

Seven pre-prepared gates (some marker must be placed at the gate’s intended location) at a distance of up to five miles from each other are activated, allowing people to move between them. The first gate appears in front of the caster. After entering a gate, roll d8 to determine the exit point; a roll of 8 means the traveller has been temporarily caught between dimensions and cannot get out for 1d6 Turns. At that point, he gets a saving throw versus Magical Device each additional Turn to emerge successfully at a random gate—if the spell hasn’t ended by that time, of course.
