# Spell Turning

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 7  
**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: 0  

Spells and spell-like effects targeted on the caster are turned back upon the original caster. The spell turns only effects that have the caster as a singular target. Area effect spells are not affected. Spell Turning also fails to stop touch range spells.

When a spell is turned, the original caster receives a saving throw if the spell normally calls for one.
