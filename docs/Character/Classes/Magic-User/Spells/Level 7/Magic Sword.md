# Magic Sword

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 7  
**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: 30'  

The caster evokes a magical sword into being and can wield it by exerting his will. While doing so, he cannot cast spells, use magic items, or move, although the spell does not end if the Magic-User’s concentration is broken; the sword simply does not attack during that Round. The sword attacks as a Warrior of one-half the Magic-User’s caster level (and always hits on a natural 19 or 20), and it can hit creatures that can ordinarily be hit only with magic weapons. The sword inflicts 1d12 damage.
