# Prismatic Wall

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 7  
**Duration**: 1 Turn/level  
**Range**: 10'  

Prismatic Wall creates a vertical, opaque wall—a shimmering, multicolored plane of light that protects the caster from all forms of attack. For all purposes this spell functions identically to the Magic-User spell [Prismatic Sphere](Prismatic%20Sphere.md), except a shimmering wall is produced rather than an opaque globe.

| Order | Color | Prismatic Sphere/Wall Effects | Negated By |
|-------|-------|-------------------------------|------------|
| 1st | Red | Stops non-magical ranged weapons.<br>Deals 10 points of damage (saving throw for half damage) | [Knock](../Level%202/Knock.md) |
| 2nd | Orange | Stops magical ranged weapons.<br>Deals 15 points of damage (saving throw for half) | [Gust of Wind](../Level%203/Gust%20of%20Wind.md) |
| 3rd | Yellow | Stops poisons, gases, and petrification.<br>Deals 20 points of damage (saving throw for half) | [Disintegrate](../Level%206/Disintegrate.md) |
| 4th | Green | Stops breath weapons. Poison (saving throw versus Poison or die) | [Passwall](../Level%205/Passwall.md) |
| 5th | Blue | Stops divination and mental attacks.<br>Turns to stone (saving throw negates) | [Magic Missile](../Level%201/Magic%20Missile.md) |
| 6th | Indigo | Stops all spells.<br>Causes insanity (saving throw negates) | [Continual Light](../Level%202/Continual%20Light.md) |
| 7th | Violet | Force shield.<br>Creatures sent to another dimension (saving throw negates) | [Dispel Magic](../Level%203/Dispel%20Magic.md)\* |

\* [Dispel Magic](../Level%203/Dispel%20Magic.md) only dispels the 7th layer and does not disrupt the entire spell.
