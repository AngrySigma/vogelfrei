# Statue

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 7  
**Duration**: 6 Turns/level  
**Range**: Touch  

A Statue spell turns the caster or a subject to solid stone, along with any garments and equipment worn or carried. The subject can see, hear, and smell normally, but it does not need to eat or breathe. Feeling is limited to those sensations that can affect the granite-hard substance of the individual’s body. Chipping is equal to a mere scratch, but breaking off one of the statue’s arms constitutes serious damage. The subject of a Statue spell can return to its normal state, act, and then return instantly to the statue state if it so desires, as long as the spell duration is in effect.
