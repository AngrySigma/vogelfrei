# Haste

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: 60'  

This spell makes creatures move and act more quickly than normal. One creature per caster level within a diameter of 60' can be affected. A Hasted creature can make double the normal number of actions. All of the Hasted creature’s modes of movement (including land movement, burrow, climb, fly, and swim) double in speed. Multiple Haste effects do not stack. Only one spell can be cast per Round while hasted.

Haste dispels and counters Slow effects.

**Slow**, the reverse of Haste, halves all attacks and movement, including spell casting such that only one spell can be cast every two Rounds.
