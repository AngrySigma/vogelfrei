# Water Breathing

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: 6 Turns/level  
**Range**: Touch  

The subject of this spell grows gills in his neck, his skin takes on a scaly texture, and he gains the ability to breathe water freely for the duration of the spell. Creatures under the influence of the spell are not granted any additional proficiency at swimming. **Air Breathing** (the reverse of Water Breathing) allows sea creatures to breathe air, but unless they already possess a means to move around on land, it does not grant them the ability to do so.
