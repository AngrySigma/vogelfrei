# Protection from Normal Missiles

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: Touch  

While under the effects of this spell, the caster or subject is completely unharmed by small and non-magical missiles. Only the subject receives this protection, and it does not extend to large hurled boulders such as those that giants employ, or enchanted arrows. The spell conveys no protection whatsoever against spells, including spells with missile-like qualities.
