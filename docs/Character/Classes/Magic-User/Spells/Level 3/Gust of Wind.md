# Gust of Wind

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: 1 Round  
**Range**: 10'/level  

This spell summons forth a powerful blast of air, forceful enough to blow out unprotected flames. The wind is strong enough to hurl a small flying creature backwards by 1d6 × 10', and will hold medium-sized creatures back from moving.
