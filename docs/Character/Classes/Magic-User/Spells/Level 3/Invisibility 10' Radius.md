# Invisibility 10' Radius

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: 1 Turn/level  
**Range**: 10' Radius Area  

The caster’s rage is so great that the light is terrified on a grand scale, and any beings, and their carried equipment, within 10' of the caster at the time of the casting become invisible as per the [Invisibility](../Level%202/Invisibility.md) spell. After the spell is cast, those affected do not have to remain close to the caster; it is as if individual Invisibility spells had been cast on each of them.
