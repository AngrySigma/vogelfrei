# Dispel Magic

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: Instantaneous  
**Range**: 120'  

As a Magic-User grows in knowledge and power, he becomes able to dismiss magic as well as conjure it. This spell empowers the proper forces of existence to expel the foul energies known as magic as if they never were. The spell affects a 20' cubic area. All spells in that area that are currently in effect are automatically negated. If cast in the same Round in combat as a spell from an enemy combatant, Dispel Magic can be used to disrupt and negate that spell.

All magical items within the area of effect are temporarily disenchanted. They regain their enchantment after 2d6 Turns, but one-use items such as potions and scrolls (but not spellbooks) are permanently nullified.

There is one important exception—a Magic-User’s Dispel Magic has no effect on Cleric spells.

Dispel Magic negates magical curses or diseases for 2d6 Turns only.
