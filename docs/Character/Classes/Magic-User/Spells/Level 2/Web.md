# Web

**Duration**: 2 Turns/level  
**Range**: 5'/level  

This spell causes a large volume of sticky, disgusting strands of goo to shoot forth from the caster’s mouth into an area approximately 20 feet square. Creatures caught within the web become entangled among the gluey fibers. Entangled creatures cannot move, but can break loose depending on their Strength. Any being with Strength in a human range can break free of the webs in 2d4 Turns. Creatures of higher Strength or magically augmented Strength above 18 can break free in 4 Rounds. Creatures larger than ogre size can rip through the web at will. The strands of a Web spell are flammable. All creatures within flaming webs take 1d6 points of fire damage from the flames for 2 Rounds. After this time, all surviving creatures are free of the webs.
