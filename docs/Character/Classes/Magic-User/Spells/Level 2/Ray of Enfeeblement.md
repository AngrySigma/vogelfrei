# Ray of Enfeeblement

**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: 20'  

When this spell is cast, a coruscating ray shoots from the caster’s hand. The target of the spell suffers a penalty to his Strength equal to 25%, +2% per caster level beyond level 3. This penalty applies equally to melee and missile damage inflicted by an affected creature as well. A successful saving throw versus Magic negates the effect.
