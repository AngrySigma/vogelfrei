# Wall of Fog

**Duration**: 5 Rounds +1 Round/level  
**Range**: 30'  

The caster of this spell creates an opaque, fog-like vapor in a 20' cube area per caster level. All beings caught within the vapor cannot see beyond 2'. Strong winds of natural or magical origin can dissipate the Wall of Fog before its duration has expired.
