# Wizard Lock

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 2  
**Duration**: Permanent  
**Range**: Touch  

Some doors are never meant to be opened. This spell magically holds shut a door, gate, window, or shutter of wood, metal, or stone. The magic affects the portal just as if it were securely closed and normally locked. A [Knock](Knock.md) spell or Dispel Magic spell can negate a Wizard Lock spell, but the Wizard Lock will take effect when a portal opened with a Knock is closed again.
