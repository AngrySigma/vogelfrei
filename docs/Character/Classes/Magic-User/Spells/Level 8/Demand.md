# Demand

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 8  
**Duration**: Permanent until Triggered  
**Range**: See Below  

Personal objects, bodily refuse such as hair or nail clippings, or even freely given gifts are dangerous in the hands of a wizard. Demand allows the caster to mentally contact the previous owner of such an object, provided the mage has it in his possession, delivering both a message and a [Suggestion](../Level%203/Suggestion.md) (as per the spell) unless the target makes a saving throw versus Magic.
