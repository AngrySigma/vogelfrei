# Permanency

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 8  
**Duration**: Permanent  
**Range**: Touch  

The Permanency spell makes other spell effects permanent. It is also used to lock the enchantments of a magic item permanently into the item, such as magical items, wands, and staves. Certain spells can be made permanent upon the caster or another creature, and other spells can be made permanent only if cast upon an area or an object. Each use of this spell requires the caster to make a saving throw versus Magic; failure permanently reduces the caster’s Toughness by 1 point.
