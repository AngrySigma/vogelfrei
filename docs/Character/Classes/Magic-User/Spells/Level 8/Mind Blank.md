# Mind Blank

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 8  
**Duration**: 1 Turn/level  
**Range**: 30'  

The subject is protected from all devices and spells that detect, influence, or read emotions or thoughts. This spell protects against all mind-affecting spells and effects as well as information gathering by divination spells or effects. In the case of scrying that scans an area the creature is in, such as a crystal ball, the spell works, but the subject of the Mind Blank spell is simply not detected. Scrying attempts that are targeted specifically at the subject do not work at all.
