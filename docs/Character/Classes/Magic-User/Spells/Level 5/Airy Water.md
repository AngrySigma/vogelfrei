# Airy Water

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 5  
**Duration**: 1 Turn/level  
**Range**: 0  

The caster of this spell creates a bubble of altered water in a body of water (or water-based medium). Within this bubble there is a breathable atmosphere. The bubble sinks in water; the centre of the bubble is on the caster and moves with him. The area of effect is either a 20' radius globe, or a 40' radius hemisphere, caster’s choice. Aquatic creatures cannot swim while in this bubble, as it is only slightly denser than air. Likewise, water breathing creatures cannot breathe within the area of effect. Intelligent aquatic creatures will not enter the area of effect by mistake.
