# Transmute Rock to Mud

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 5  
**Duration**: Permanent  
**Range**: 120'  

This spell turns 3,000 square feet of rock 10' deep into mud for 3d6 days. Any beings passing through the mud have their movement reduced by 90%. **Transmute Mud to Rock** (the reverse of Transmute Rock to Mud) changes an equal volume of mud described above into rock. This alteration is permanent unless the reverse spell is cast on the altered mud or rock.
