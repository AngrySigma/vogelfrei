# Cloudkill

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 5  
**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: 30'  

Casting this spell allows the smoke emitted from the infernal fires of Hell to spew forth onto the Earth to cause destruction and suffering. A 20' cube cloud of poison gas forms which moves at a rate of 10' per Round under the control of the caster (so long as he concentrates on it). The gas kills any creatures of 3 or fewer Hit Dice or levels it comes in contact with if they fail a saving throw versus Poison; creatures having 4 or more Hit Dice or levels are unaffected. The cloud persists for the entire duration even if the caster ceases to concentrate upon it, in which case the cloud also remains immobile for the remainder of the spell’s duration.
