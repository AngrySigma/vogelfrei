# Passwall

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 5  
**Duration**: 3 Turns  
**Range**: 30'  

The caster creates a passage through wooden, plaster, or stone walls, but not through metal or other harder materials. The passage is 10' deep with a 5' diameter.
