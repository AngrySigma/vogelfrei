# Feeblemind

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 5  
**Duration**: Permanent  
**Range**: 120'  

This spell turns the neural pathways of the target into a superconducting network of thought. In most targets, this overloads their brain. If the target creature fails a saving throw versus Magic (with a –4 penalty!), it becomes a mental invalid. The affected creature is unable to speak, cast spells, understand language, or communicate coherently. The subject remains in this state until a [Dispel Magic](../Level%203/Dispel%20Magic.md) or [Remove Curse](../../../Cleric/Miracles/Level%203/Remove%20Curse.md) spell is used to cancel the effect of the Feeblemind.

However, if the target succeeds with his saving throw, he is considered to be under the effect of a [Haste](../Level%203/Haste.md) spell for as many Rounds as the caster has levels.
