# Interposing Hand

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 5  
**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: 10'/level  

Interposing Hand creates a large magic hand that appears between the caster and one opponent. This floating, disembodied hand then moves to remain between the two, regardless of where the caster moves or how the opponent tries to get around it. The hand does not pursue an opponent, however. An Interposing Hand has as many Wounds as the caster when undamaged. It has an AC of 12. Any creature weighing 2,000 pounds or less that tries to push past the hand is slowed to half its normal speed.
