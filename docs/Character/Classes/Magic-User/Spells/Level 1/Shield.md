# Shield

**Class**: Magic-User  
**Level**: 1  
**Duration**: 2 Turns  
**Range**: Touch  

Magic-Users are masters of matter and as such can command objects racing towards them to stop. Against missile attacks, the spell grants the caster a **+5 bonus to Armour Class**, and a **+3 bonus to Armour Class** against all other attacks. Even when an attack still lands, it does one less point of damage than it otherwise would have.
