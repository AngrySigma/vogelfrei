# Delay Poison

**Class**: Cleric  
**Level**: 2  
**Duration**: Instantaneous  
**Range**: Touch  

This spell counteracts the work of poison already done, weakening it, giving its victim a new chance at life. Any character that has succumbed to poison within the previous 24 hours can immediately make a new saving throw versus Poison to resist its effects when this spell is cast.
