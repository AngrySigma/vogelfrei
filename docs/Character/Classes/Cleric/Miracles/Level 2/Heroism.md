# Heroism

**Class**: Cleric  
**Level**: 2  
**Duration**: 1 Round/level  
**Range**: Touch  

This spell grants the subject 1d4 levels’ worth of Wounds and saving throws (and, for Warriors, Weapon Skill and Ballistic Skill) for the duration of the spell.

When the spell ends, if the subject has more Wounds than when the spell was cast, his Wounds return to their previous level. If the subject has less than or equal to the number of Wounds, then there is no adjustment once the spell ends.
