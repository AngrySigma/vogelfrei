# Resist Cold

**Class**: Cleric  
**Level**: 2  
**Duration**: 1 Turn/level  
**Range**: Touch  

While under the effects of this spell, a character or monster is unharmed by freezing (non-magical) cold, and receives a bonus of +2 to all saving throws versus cold-based magical or breath attacks. In addition, 1 point of damage is subtracted from each die of damage dealt by a cold-based attack. Each die will still inflict a minimum of 1 point of damage.
