# Resist Fire

**Class**: Cleric  
**Level**: 2  
**Duration**: 1 Turn/level  
**Range**: Touch  

While under the effects of this spell, a character or monster is unharmed by intense (non-magical) heat, and he receives a bonus of +3 to all saving throws versus heat-based magical or breath attacks. In addition, 1 point of damage is subtracted from each die of damage dealt by a heat-based attack. Each die will still inflict a minimum of 1 point of damage.
