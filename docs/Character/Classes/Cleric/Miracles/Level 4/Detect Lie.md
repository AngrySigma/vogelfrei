# Detect Lie

**Class**: Cleric  
**Level**: 4  
**Duration**: Instantaneous  
**Range**: 0  

This spell allows the caster to determine if any one statement made to him in the past hour was the truth or a lie. Honest mistakes are not detected as lies, nor are untrue statements made by charmed or bewitched individuals, for they know not what they say.
