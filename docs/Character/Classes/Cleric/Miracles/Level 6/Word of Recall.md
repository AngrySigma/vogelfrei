# Word of Recall

**Class**: Cleric  
**Level**: 6  
**Duration**: Instantaneous  
**Range**: 0  

The casting of this spell summons the very essence of his deity to protect him, and this divine force removes the caster from his current location and delivers him instantly to his sanctuary. The caster must designate the sanctuary when preparing the spell, and it must be a very familiar place. Any distance can be traveled with no chance of error. In addition to himself, the caster can transport an additional 25 pounds per level of experience.
