# Dispel Magic

**Class**: Cleric  
**Level**: 3  
**Duration**: Instantaneous  
**Range**: 120'  

Magic is unnatural and unwanted in the intended order of things. This spell empowers the proper forces of existence to expel the foul energies known as magic as if they never were. The spell affects a 20' cubic area. All spells in that area that are currently in effect are automatically negated. If cast in the same Round in combat as a spell from an enemy combatant, Dispel Magic can be used to disrupt and negate that spell.

Monsters with magical ability or able to create magical effects (but not Magic-Users) lose the ability to use these powers for 2d6 Turns if they are within the area of effect and fail a saving throw versus Magic.

All magical items within the area of effect are temporarily disenchanted. They regain their enchantment after 2d6 Turns. If the caster casts Dispel Magic as a touch effect, then any item so touched is permanently disenchanted.

Dispel Magic negates magical curses or diseases for 2d6 Turns only.
