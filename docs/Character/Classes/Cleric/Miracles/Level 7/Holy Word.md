# Holy Word

**Class**: Cleric  
**Level**: 7  
**Duration**: See Below  
**Range**: 0  

The language of gods is not one for mortal ears. In fact, it is the speaking of these words that is behind the power of Clerical magic. Some words though, are more devastating than others. The most destructive word is the true name of the Cleric’s god, spoken as the god would speak it. The intonation of this *Holy Word* utterly banishes creatures not native to the caster’s plane, hurling them back to their respective planes of existence. Additionally, other creatures will suffer effects from the *Holy Word* in accordance with the creature’s Hit Dice:

| Hit Dice | Effect                                                |
|----------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| < 4      | Killed                                                |
| 4–7      | Paralyzed 1d4 Turns                                   |
| 8–11     | Slowed 2d4 Rounds                                     |
| 12+      | Deafened 1d4 Rounds, –2 to hit, Movement –25%         |

All creatures, save angels or other legitimately holy beings, priests, Clerics and devout followers of the caster’s religion, within 60' of the caster are affected.

The reverse of *Holy Word*, **Unholy Word**, has the same effect save that demons and unholy creatures are not affected.
