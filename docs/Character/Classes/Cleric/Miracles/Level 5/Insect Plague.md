# Insect Plague

**Class**: Cleric  
**Level**: 5  
**Duration**: 1 Turn/level  
**Range**: 360'  

The caster summons a swarm of locusts in a 60' diameter. The swarm causes creatures occupying its area to flee if they are 2 or less Hit Dice. This spell does not function when cast underground. The caster can control the swarm to move 20' in a Round. The caster must concentrate for the duration of the spell to maintain control over the swarm, and if the swarm leaves the range the caster loses control of it. The caster also loses control of the swarm if he is successfully attacked.
