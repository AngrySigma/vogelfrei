# Scout

**Class**: [Ranger](../index.md)

**Status**: Brass

**Skills**: Any non-special skills

Scouts range ahead of travellers and armies, finding routes, watching unseen, and reading the signs left by those who passed before them.

| Level | Progression |
| ----- | ----------- |
| 1     | Wayfinding |
| 2     | +1 BS |
| 3     | Awareness |
| 4     | +1-in-6 Bushcraft; Wilderness Stealth |
| 5     | +1 BS |
| 6     | Pursuit and Tracking |

!!! tip "Trait"
    **Wayfinding:** When the Scout navigates for the party, reduce the terrain's [chance of becoming lost](../../../../Adventuring/Wilderness%20Travel.md#losing-direction) by 1-in-6. A chance reduced to zero requires no lost roll. The navigator's normal Bushcraft roll still applies before any lost roll. Multiple Scouts do not combine this reduction.

### Awareness

From level 3, reduce the Scout's [Surprise](../../../../Encounters/Surprise.md) range by 1. Use the party's roll: ordinarily, the Scout is surprised only on a 1, and can act on a 2 even when companions are surprised. Against a prepared ambush, the Scout is surprised on 1–3 instead of 1–4.

### Wilderness Stealth

At level 4, the Scout gains +1-in-6 [Bushcraft](../../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#bushcraft) in addition to their normal Skill Point. They may use Bushcraft in place of [Stealth](../../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#stealth) in the wilderness, under the usual conditions for that skill.

### Pursuit and Tracking

From level 6, the Scout adds their Bushcraft rating to [pursuit rolls](../../../../Encounters/Combat%20Actions.md#flee) in the wilderness, whether chasing or escaping. A rating of 4-in-6 grants +4, for example.

The Scout searches for tracks as part of normal exploration, without taking extra time. Use Bushcraft to find or interpret an uncertain trail.
