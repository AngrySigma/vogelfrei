# Rogue

Rogues are defined by their adaptability and their lack of official social standing. A Rogue may have been anything before taking to the road, and answers to no guild, temple or banner.

### Starting traits:

- **Skill Points Progression:** Rogues get Skill Points, which can be used to improve their skills. Characters start with the base proficiency of 1-in-6 that every character has and can spend Skill Points to improve them; each point raises one skill's chance by 1-in-6.

- **Skills:** [Architecture](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#architecture), [Bushcraft](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#bushcraft), [Climb](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#climb), [Doors](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#doors), [Languages](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#languages), [Search](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#search), [Sleight of Hand](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#sleight-of-hand), [Stealth](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#stealth), [Tinkering](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#tinkering)

!!! tip "Sneak Attack"
    When a Rogue strikes a target that is unaware of them or otherwise Surprised — as when they attack straight out of [Stealth](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#stealth) — they roll the weapon's damage dice twice and add both results together.

!!! tip "Skill Specialist"
    A Rogue is the trained specialist the skill rules allow for: they may use the [Climb](../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#climb) skill even while encumbered.

## Careers

The Rogue has no careers. A Rogue is a class in itself.
