# Duellist

**Class**: [Warrior](../index.md)

**Status**: Silver

**Combat Skills**: WS

!!! tip "Trait"
    When making a [Counterattack](../../../../Encounters/Combat%20Actions.md#counterattack), the Duellist retains their Weapon Skill bonus to AC against the opponent they counterattack. Other attackers ignore that bonus as usual.
