# Apothecary

**Class**: [Academic](../index.md)

**Status**: Brass

**Skills**: [Bushcraft](../../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#bushcraft), [Languages](../../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#languages), [Lore (Herbalism)](../../../../Adventuring/Skills.md#lore)

| Level | Progression        |
| ----- | ------------------ |
| 1     | One known potion recipe |
| 2     | Research one recipe; +2 Poison save |
| 3     | Research one recipe; research potions replicating first-level Magic-User spells |
| 4     | Research one recipe; +2 Breath save |
| 5     | Research one recipe; +2 Magic save |
| 6     | Research one recipe; research potions replicating second-level Magic-User spells |

### Recipes

An Apothecary begins with one potion recipe, chosen with the Referee. At each subsequent level, they may research one new recipe without paying research costs. The research still takes the normal time and must be completed before the recipe can be used.

Early recipes describe herbal preparations; the Referee determines their ingredients, effects, and preparation times. From level 3, an Apothecary may research potions that replicate first-level Magic-User spells. At level 6, this extends to second-level spells. Only spells that affect a person can be made into potions, with the effect applying to the drinker.

Researching a recipe that replicates a spell takes the same time as [researching that spell](../../Magic-User/Special%20Rules/Magical%20Activities.md#time-of-magical-activities). Brewing it follows the normal [potion-making rules](../../Magic-User/Special%20Rules/Magical%20Activities.md#creating-a-potion), but knowing the recipe replaces the requirement to cast the spell. Free research does not cover the cost of brewing potions.
