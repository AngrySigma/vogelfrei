# Monsters and NPCs

Player Characters attack with Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill and save by their class tables. Everything else in the world uses the two conventions on this page.

## How Monsters Attack

A monster adds its **Hit Dice** to its attack rolls, in place of the Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill and ability modifiers a Character would apply. A creature whose Hit Dice carry a bonus (2+1, 4+2, and so on) attacks as one Hit Die higher.

A monster's AC is part of its description — as noted in [Combat Actions](Combat%20Actions.md), it may run from very high for small, quick creatures to almost nothing for the colossal. Remember also that monsters and unintelligent creatures have no Stamina: all damage they take goes straight to Wounds (see [Damage](../Adventuring/Hazards/Damage.md)).

## How Monsters Save

Monsters save using the row matching their Hit Dice on the table below. Save categories work exactly as for Characters, including the left-to-right precedence rule from [Class and Career](../Character/Class%20and%20Career.md).

| Hit Dice | Paralyze | Poison | Breath | Device | Magic |
| -------- | -------- | ------ | ------ | ------ | ----- |
| Level 0 | 16 | 14 | 17 | 15 | 18 |
| 1–3 | 14 | 12 | 15 | 13 | 16 |
| 4–6 | 12 | 10 | 13 | 11 | 14 |
| 7–9 | 10 | 8 | 10 | 9 | 12 |
| 10–12 | 8 | 6 | 8 | 7 | 10 |
| 13–15 | 6 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 8 |
| 16–18 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 6 |
| 19+ | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |

A creature's description may instead direct it to a specific class's saving throw table; that always takes precedence.

## Level 0 Characters

Everyone in the world has a class, but most people have no career advances at all — the innkeeper, the ploughman, the tax collector. These level 0 characters:

- attack with **no** Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill bonus — only their ability modifiers apply;

- save on the **Level 0** row of the table above, unless their class description says otherwise;

- are still trained, intelligent beings, so they may have a sliver of Stamina if the Referee thinks their life has earned them one — a veteran watchman, yes; a pastry cook, no.

Classed NPCs — a level 2 Road Warden, a level 4 Witch — use their class and career tables exactly as a player Character would.
