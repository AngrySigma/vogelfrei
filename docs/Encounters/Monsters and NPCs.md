# Monsters and NPCs

Player Characters attack with Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill and save by their class tables. Everything else in the world uses the two conventions on this page.

## How Monsters Attack

A monster adds its **Hit Dice** to its attack rolls, in place of the Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill a Character would apply. Like anyone else, it adds nothing to a firearm attack. A creature whose Hit Dice carry a bonus (2+1, 4+2, and so on) attacks as one Hit Die higher.

A monster's AC is part of its description — as noted in [Combat Actions](Combat%20Actions.md), it may run from very high for small, quick creatures to almost nothing for the colossal. Natural AC is not an Armour Rating, so weapons' armour modifiers do not apply against it, unless the description says how much of it is hide or scale. Remember also that monsters and unintelligent creatures have no Stamina: all damage they take goes straight to Wounds (see [Damage](../Adventuring/Hazards/Damage.md)).

## How Monsters Save

Monsters save using the row matching their Hit Dice on the table below. Save categories work exactly as for Characters, including the left-to-right precedence rule from [Class and Career](../Character/Class%20and%20Career.md).

| Hit Dice | Paralyse | Poison | Breath | Device | Magic |
| -------- | -------- | ------ | ------ | ------ | ----- |
| Level 0 | 16 | 14 | 17 | 15 | 18 |
| 1–3 | 14 | 12 | 15 | 13 | 16 |
| 4–6 | 12 | 10 | 13 | 11 | 14 |
| 7–9 | 10 | 8 | 10 | 9 | 12 |
| 10–12 | 8 | 6 | 8 | 7 | 10 |
| 13–15 | 6 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 8 |
| 16–18 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 6 |
| 19+ | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |

A creature's description may instead direct it to a specific class's saving throw table; that always takes precedence.

## Level 0 Characters

Everyone in the world has a class, but most people have no career advances at all — the innkeeper, the ploughman, the tax collector. These level 0 characters:

- attack with a plain **d20**, with no Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill bonus;

- save on the **Level 0** row of their class's saving throw table, which is the level 1 row two points worse; if the Referee has not settled the class, the **Level 0** row of the table above serves;

- have the Wounds on the **Level 0** row of their class table, and **no Stamina**: every blow goes straight to Wounds.

As a rule of thumb, a peasant has **6 Wounds**, a townsman or a clerk **5**, and someone who has seen hard service — an old soldier, a veteran watchman — **7**. When in doubt, an unlevelled man met in a dungeon has 6. Toughness counts only if the Referee has bothered to give the NPC ability scores.

There are no level 0 Magic-Users: whoever has learned to cast a spell has the first level of the class.

Classed NPCs — a level 2 Road Warden, a level 4 Witch — use their class and career tables exactly as a player Character would, Stamina included.

!!! note "Monsters"
    This book does not stat monsters. Their Hit Dice, Wounds and abilities are the Referee's to set, from a bestiary or from imagination; the conventions on this page say only how they attack and save.
