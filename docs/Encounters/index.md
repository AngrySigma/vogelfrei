# Encounters

Encounters are any situation where the player characters meet other characters or creatures in uncertain circumstances. Attack? Parley? Run? Sneak around? All, and more, are possible actions and outcomes...

Encounter pipeline is as follows:

1. [Determine if any party is surprised](Surprise.md)

2. [Determine Encounter Distance](Encounter%20Distance.md)

3. [Determine Reaction](Reaction%20Roll.md)

4. [Roll for initiative at the start of each Round](Initiative.md)

5. [Move to Combat](Combat%20Actions.md), minding [Measure and Reach](Measure%20and%20Reach.md)

When any side has gained the upper hand, you may need the following rules:

1. [Do a Morale Check](Morale.md)

2. [Flee!](Combat%20Actions.md#flee)

For how the other side of the table attacks and saves, see [Monsters and NPCs](Monsters%20and%20NPCs.md).
