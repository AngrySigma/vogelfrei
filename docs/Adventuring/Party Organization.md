# Party Organization

An expedition that sorts itself out before the torches are lit moves twice as fast at the table and dies half as often in play. The following habits are recommended for every party.

## Marching Order

Before setting out, the players should agree on the normal arrangement of their Characters when exploring — who walks in front, who guards the rear, where the lantern and the Characters who cannot afford to be hit are placed. This is known as the party's marching order.

A group may define several standing marching orders for common situations — normal exploration, opening a door, searching a room, battle — so that when something happens, nobody has to ask where everyone was standing. The Referee is entitled to hold the party to its declared order.

## The Caller

If the players wish, they may nominate one of their number as the group's spokesperson, known as the caller. The caller informs the Referee of the actions and movements of the party as a whole, after discussion with the other players. Delegating this to one voice — rather than six people addressing the Referee at once — speeds up play considerably.

The caller's Character usually acts as the party leader, and is best placed near the front of the marching order. A high Leadership does not hurt.

## The Mapper

One player should keep a map of the areas explored, drawn from the Referee's descriptions. Monsters encountered, traps found, clues to puzzles, and promising unexplored passages should all be noted as the map grows. The map is the party's memory: no mapping is possible while [fleeing](../Encounters/Combat%20Actions.md#flee), which is one more reason not to flee blindly.

## Dividing the Spoils

The spoils of an expedition are divided between the surviving Characters however the players agree — but agree *before* the expedition, not over the open chest.

- **Coin and treasure** is typically divided evenly among the player Characters, after any retainer shares are paid out. Retainers and henchmen receive the fractional shares negotiated at hire; see [Retainers](../Retainers/index.md) for how fractional shares are calculated.

- **Magic items** cannot be split. One common method: each interested player rolls a die, and picks are made from highest roll to lowest. Another is rotation — whoever picked last time picks last this time. Whatever the method, settle it in town, in daylight, sober.
