- **Critical — Rules (source lines 46–85):** The Form table cannot be rolled as written: its two identically named Roll columns contain overlapping first-column results (3 and 3–4), blanks, and unexplained second rolls. Rebuild it with explicit dice and one unambiguous result path.
- **Critical — Rules (source lines 160–178):** Every armour power subtracts dice from AC, which makes the entity easier to hit under the book’s ascending-AC system; change these to AC bonuses or fixed ascending values.
- **Critical — Philosophy/serious content (source lines 393, 429–431):** Random results can erase another player’s character without a save and mandate sexual assault/pregnancy. These remove player agency far beyond ordinary OSR risk and require explicit table consent; replace them with consequences that preserve meaningful choices.
- **High — Epoch (source lines 18, 66, 126, 272, 290–296, 405–447):** “space and time,” antimatter, neon, psionics, radiation/radioactivity, collective unconscious, and branching timelines import 19th–20th-century scientific/psychological vocabulary into the 17th-century setting; recast these as period occult or theological concepts.
- **High — Rules (source lines 180–358):** The d100 power list relies heavily on undefined spells/effects (for example *Cloudkill*, *Lost Dweomer*, *Radiation Attack*, and *Time Stop*) and never actually says to roll d100, so many generated entities cannot be adjudicated from this book.
- **High — Rules (source line 373):** The creature’s Great Margin uses the Magic-User’s “Hit Dice,” while every other domination calculation uses caster level and player classes do not define personal Hit Dice; use level consistently.
- **High — Balance (source lines 43, 76–87, 377, 403–447):** A failed first-level casting can produce campaign-ending effects, including permanent Referee replacement or a worldwide 50-foot flood, with no scale tied to caster level; reserve Abstract Forms for higher-level/researched magic or constrain their scope.
- **High — Rules (source line 444):** “Segments” do not exist in the current initiative rules, and the result alternates between full Rounds and Segments; rewrite it using the game’s defined Round/Initiative sequence.
- **Medium — Rules (source lines 365–369):** The normal control duration says the victory margin determines “how many d10s” but never states the formula, while “double a Great Margin” is also ambiguous; give exact thresholds and dice expressions.
