- **High — OSR philosophy (source line 9):** Awarding no XP for sneaking past, tricking, or negotiating with a threat, while awarding it for magical pacification, discourages player-skill solutions and incentivizes force; count any meaningful defeat/overcoming of a threat or leave enemy XP entirely to the Referee.
- **Medium — Rules (source line 69):** “Any Experience Points over half-way to the next level earned from a single session is lost” does not define whether the cap is half the next level's threshold, half the interval between levels, or only XP earned after levelling; state the exact retained-XP ceiling.
- **High — Rules (source lines 73–75):** Career/class changing does not say which former traits, spell access, saves, or progression benefits remain, and “retaining Skills only” conflicts with gaining multiple first-level careers later; define what is retained and how total level selects future advances.
