- **High — Epoch (source lines 98–145):** The engineering list mixes late-period rarities with later weapons. The Met dates the familiar revolving pepperbox to 1842–47 ([collection record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/24941)); a museum duck’s-foot example is dated 1780 ([Hampshire Cultural Trust](https://collections.hampshireculture.org.uk/object/pistol-60-bore-flintlock-ducks-foot-pistol-spread-shot-four-barrels-firing-together-belt)); and the cited self-rotation “using shot inertia” is a recoil-operated idea not suitable to a 17th-century arm. Remove or explicitly make these unique fantasy inventions rather than purchasable period gear.
- **High — Material/Rules (source lines 178–181):** Duck-foot and hand-mortar effects say targets take damage “assuming no Armor is penetrated,” the reverse of the apparent intent; neither defines how armor penetration is tested, and the hand mortar says a shield modifies a save without giving the modifier.
- **High — Rules (source line 191):** Reload time has no minimum. BS + Agility can reach or exceed 10, producing zero/negative-round reloads; set a floor and state rounding for later halving/increases.
- **Medium — Material (source line 164):** Blanket “ignore 5 Armor Rating” makes buff coats through three-quarter armor identically worthless and reduces even full plate to +1, although 17th-century cuirassier armor was specifically thickened and proofed against firearms ([Met overview](https://www.metmuseum.org/it/essays/fashion-in-european-armor-1600-1700)). Model proofed plate separately or qualify penetration by firearm/range.
- **Medium — Rules/Balance (source line 166):** Every discharge forces every enemy of Morale 7 or less to check, with no hearing range, familiarity exception, or once-per-encounter limit; massed shots can trigger unlimited battlefield-wide checks.
- **Low — Grammar (source lines 162, 170, 178–181, 195, 199, 223):** Multiple agreement/article errors (“a -1 penalty,” “a variable number,” “amount ... equals,” “cannot,” “Wet conditions ... quadruple,” final period) need copy-editing.
